# Hooks: the text that decides whether anyone watches

The hook is the only text most viewers read. It has about one second to make the footage make sense
and make the viewer need what comes next. Treat writing hooks as the main job; the render is the
cheap part. Same footage + 5 hooks = 5 reels, and a hook variant re-renders in seconds, so **iterate
hooks before footage**.

The engine places, sizes and fits the text (TikTok Sans, inside the safe band). You choose the words,
the position and, where the template allows, a second line.

## Rules

- **Short.** Under ~80 characters, 3–7 words per line, at most 3 lines on a phone. Readable in 1 s.
  (Wall-of-text is the exception: its *first line* is the hook, the rest is meant to be re-read.)
- **Native, not ad-speak.** Lowercase, first person or POV / "when". "i can't believe this is free"
  beats "Discover our revolutionary solution".
- **One idea.** Pain, or aha, or curiosity. Not all three.
- **Match the footage's emotion** (table below). A shocked face under a deadpan line reads as a mistake.
- **Specific beats clever.** Numbers and moments ("i logged 12 cravings today and gave in to zero",
  "when you said 'one video' 3 hours ago") beat vague lines ("when life is hard").
- **Don't name the product.** "this app" opens a curiosity gap; the comments ask "what app??" and the
  demo answers. Name it only when the brand itself is the hook, or late (a reveal card).
- **Truthful.** Nothing the brief can't back: numbers, "free", "#1", results, outcomes.
- **Vary formats** across a set (at least 3) so the algorithm can find a winner.

## Emotion → hook pairing

Pick the emotion first, then the line (and, for reaction formats, the clip). Ranked roughly by how
often they stop the scroll:

| Footage emotion | Pairs with | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| shocked / surprised / amazed | a surprising reveal, number or capability | why did nobody tell me this app exists |
| crying / sad | relatable pain, then relief | me after losing another evening to reels |
| angry / frustrated | problem-agitate, "they don't want you to know" (keep it true) | i paid for 3 apps that do less than this |
| laughing / playful | an absurd situation, a self-roast | my phone making me do pushups to open instagram |
| confused / curious | myth-busting, "wait, how?" | wait you can just do this?? |
| excited / happy | wins, before/after | my screen time before this app vs after |
| neutral / confident | deadpan understatement | anyway i fixed my sleep |

## Formats

| Format | Pattern | Example (habit-lock app) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POV | `pov: <situation the product changes>` | pov: your phone won't open tiktok until you work out |
| When / me when | `when <relatable pain moment>` | when you said "one video" 3 hours ago |
| Discovery | `why did nobody tell me <X> exists` | why did nobody tell me this app exists |
| Disbelief | `this <product type> just <surprising action>` | this app just made me do pushups to open instagram |
| Before / after | `my <metric> before this vs after` | my screen time before this app vs after |
| Callout | `<audience> you need this` | college students you NEED this |
| Confession | `i'm embarrassed how much this helped` | i'm embarrassed how well this worked |
| Question | `is it normal to <pain>?` | is it normal to lose whole evenings to reels? |
| Us vs them | `everyone: <common advice> / me: <the product way>` | everyone: just use willpower. me: |
| Stakes | `<thing> was about to <bad outcome>` | my finals were about to cook me |
| Nobody talks about | `nobody tells you <hidden truth>` | nobody tells you reels are built to never end |
| I wish | `i wish someone told me <lesson> sooner` | i wish someone told me to lock my apps sooner |
| Listicle | `<N> <things> that feel illegal to know` | 3 apps that feel illegal to know |
| Outcome flex | `<enviable concrete outcome> but no yapping` | fixed my sleep in a week but no yapping |
| Community voice | `somebody find me the developer of this app` | please tag the developer bc they lowkey saved my life |

## Per template

| Template | Hook pattern | Second line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reaction-demo` | `--hook` over a ≤ 3 s reaction; the demo must pay it off within ~2 s of the cut | `--hook2`: a payoff over the demo ("12/12 resisted"), shorter than the hook; rewards finishing |
| `wall-of-text` | the first line of `--text` ("pov:", "storytime:", "nobody talks about how…"); the paragraph should take longer to read than the footage lasts | `--text2`: the reveal ("the app is called x btw") or nothing, and let the comments ask |
| `listicle` | number + category + a forbidden/secret feeling; 3 or 5 items, best last (`--numbering down`) | `--cta` that baits comments ("which one are you trying first?") |
| `no-yapping` | `[enviable, concrete outcome] but no yapping`; steps are 2–5 word lowercase imperatives | the **last step** is the flex and holds to the end |
| `greenscreen-reaction` | community voice, third person, like a fan post | the creator's own voice is the second line |
| `talking-head-captions` | `--hook` title = the promise in under ~10 words; don't repeat the first spoken line | the captions deliver it; start on the strongest spoken line (`--trim`) |
| `before-after` | time + transformation: "6 months of fixing my posture" | `--payoff` when the after appears ("and i only changed one thing") |

## Writing a set

1. From the brief's **pain** and **aha**, draft 2–3 hooks per concept (more when the user wants
   many variants). Use the formats the chosen template is built for.
2. Check each against the rules: length, truth, emotion match, product unnamed. At least 3 formats
   across the set.
3. Show a table: # · hook · format · footage / emotion. Let the user cut, edit or add.
4. Make one reel per kept hook (see variants.md).

## Anti-patterns

- "Introducing X, the all-in-one platform for…": ad-speak, instant scroll.
- "You won't BELIEVE…" clickbait with no specifics.
- Hashtags or emoji in the overlay. They belong in the post caption (TikTok Sans has no emoji glyphs).
- Invented stats: "saves 10 hours a week" when nothing says so.
- Describing the feature instead of the feeling ("app with lock screen feature").
- Walls of text in a hook slot: if it needs 4+ lines, it is two hooks (or a wall-of-text reel).
- Anything implying a creator is AI or a paid actor reading a script.
- Copying a viral creator's line word for word. Borrow the structure, write your own.

## Length and pacing

| Reel length | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 4–10 s | one idea, highest completion, loops: before-after, a tight reaction-demo, short wall-of-text |
| **10–15 s** | **the default**: reaction-demo (~3 s reaction + 7–12 s demo), wall-of-text, a 3-item listicle |
| 15–30 s | 5-item listicles, greenscreen reactions, short talking heads, most no-yapping tutorials |
| 30–45 s | talking-head storytime or advice, a dense no-yapping tutorial; only if every second earns it |
| 45 s+ | avoid, especially for ads |

Captions need at least ~1 s + 0.3 s per word on screen (wall-of-text excepted).
